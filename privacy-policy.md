# Privacy Policy

## 1. Information We Collect
TurtleBot does not collect any personal information from users. When you interact with the bot, the only data we receive is the command you send to request a picture of a turtle. We do not collect, store, or process any user data beyond the immediate response to your request.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
The only information we process is the command you send to request a turtle picture. This command is used solely to provide you with the requested content. No other data processing or usage occurs.

## 3. Data Sharing and Disclosure
TurtleBot does not share any data with third parties. Since we do not collect or store personal information, there is nothing to disclose or share.

## 4. Data Security
We take your privacy seriously and implement security measures to protect the integrity of the service. However, as we do not collect any personal information, there is minimal risk to user privacy.

## 5. Children's Privacy
TurtleBot is not designed to collect personal information from any users, including children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has used the bot inappropriately, please contact us, and we will ensure any concerns are addressed.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, so please check back periodically. Your continued use of Turtle Picture Bot after any modifications to the policy will signify your acceptance of the changes.
